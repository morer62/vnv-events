# Mommy & Daddy Break Club · invitation campaign

VNV Events Originals | South Florida | November 26, 2026 – January 24, 2027, Eastern time

## Creative direction

**A morning of your own.** Follow the journey from reheating coffee at home to finishing a conversation at a club table. Show mornings that parents recognize, with mothers, fathers and solo guests represented. Film close details in natural light. The hero film and teaser illustrate the concept. Identify concept footage in posts as a preview until footage of a real edition exists.

Publish two organic posts per week on the club's approved social account. Use Instagram Reels and carousels as the primary formats, with vertical video suitable for Facebook or TikTok when the club has access. The schedule below is an editorial calendar. Hold a date or ticket announcement until production confirms the venue, date, ticket price, capacity, cancellation terms and checkout. A proposed January event is an internal planning target.

## Conversion and measurement

- Use **Join the free priority list** as the call to action from the first post. Link to [the club waitlist](https://vnvevents.com/vnv-events-productions/mommy-and-daddy-break/#waitlist).
- Use tagged links such as `?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=break_club_founders&utm_content=w01_thu#waitlist` when a placement supports its own URL. For a shared bio link, use `utm_content=bio`. Attribute a particular organic post only when its link is distinguishable.
- Review each week’s visits, confirmed primary members, people requested, invitations delivered, paid guests and actual attendees. Count a companion as a guest, not a second subscribed address. Compare confirmed members to landing visits, and paid reservations to delivered invitations.
- Once the venue, date, capacity, price and terms are confirmed, test checkout and the confirmation email. Plan a **48-hour member-first window** before a public ticket announcement. Publish a remaining-seat claim only from actual inventory. If the date is still pending, continue the waitlist campaign.
- Editorial review with Claude in the existing [VNV growth strategy conversation](https://claude.ai/chat/780e11f6-6721-403d-9f80-55c45d5c9fc3) informed the morning ritual, the inclusive language and the invitation-first sequence. Review confirmed demand alongside seat capacity and break-even cost before committing to the first edition.
