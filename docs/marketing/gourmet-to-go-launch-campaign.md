# Gourmet To Go — Launch Campaign

## Creative idea

**For once, I got to sit at my own table.**

The campaign follows a South Florida hostess who expects family and friends, chooses not to lose the day in the kitchen, orders Gourmet To Go, and gets to enjoy the gathering she created.

## 30-second voice-over

> Sunday's at my place. Everyone's coming. And honestly? I didn't want to spend the whole day cooking. So I ordered Gourmet To Go. It arrived beautiful, ready to heat and serve. Easy to order. Easy on the budget. And for once… I got to sit at my own table. Gourmet To Go by VNV Events. Ready when you are.

Delivery should sound conversational and warm, not like an announcer. Female voice, approximately 35–45, natural South Florida bilingual cadence without exaggerating an accent.

## Visual sequence

1. A relatable Latina hostess receives the family group message: everyone is coming Sunday.
2. She sits with coffee and orders easily from her phone.
3. Gourmet To Go arrives in a natural kraft delivery box.
4. Real product close-ups: chicken and chorizo paella and lasagna/pasticho in silver aluminum trays; Latin bites in a kraft box.
5. Family and friends arrive, hug, serve themselves and share the food.
6. The hostess is finally seated at her own table, laughing with everyone else.

## Creative guardrails

- Do not show prices or serving quantities.
- Use only the real advertised foods and packaging.
- Food trays are silver aluminum; Latin bites use a kraft box with food-safe paper.
- No generated logos or legible AI-generated interface text.
- The mood is warm, human and attainable—not cold luxury.
