# Mommy & Daddy Break Club media

The launch films portray a South Florida morning through coffee, vinyl and adult conversation. These are illustrative campaign scenes. They do not depict a confirmed venue or previous club attendees.

## Deliverables

All public media live under `/assets/break-club/`.

| Asset | Intended use |
| --- | --- |
| `club-logo.svg`, `club-logo-ink.svg` | Transparent wordmarks for a dark and light background respectively. Both have a 544 × 118 viewBox and use self-contained outlines. |
| `hero.mp4`, `hero-mobile.mp4` | Seamless 16-second silent landing film, 1440 × 810 or 960 × 540, H.264. |
| `hero-poster.webp`, `hero-poster-mobile.webp` | 1440 × 810 and 768 × 432 stills for the initial frame and reduced-motion viewing. |
| `teaser-vertical.mp4`, `teaser-poster.webp` | 720 × 1280 campaign film, 14.64 seconds. Opens with “When was your last slow morning?” and ends with the free priority-list invitation. Original instrumental, AAC stereo, integrated −16 LUFS. |

## Creation and rights record

The sunrise, water and arched-window symbol was drawn for this club. The wordmark uses outlined Instrument Serif, its italic ampersand and Manrope lettering. [Instrument Serif source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif) and [Manrope source](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope).

Five commissioned digital illustrations were created with the built-in image generator. The art direction called for a candid conversation on a peach-and-jade Florida courtyard, an espresso with a guava pastry on sunlit travertine, a turntable in morning light, a parent putting a phone face down and two parents talking beneath palms. The fictional individuals and setting are campaign imagery. The final films alternate photographic camera moves and licensed live-action footage. The vertical score is an original instrumental programmed for this release.

The moving macro inserts are edited excerpts under the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/).
