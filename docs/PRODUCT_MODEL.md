# Product Model

Avomeal products should be organized around commercial intent, not only around legacy meal-prep categories.

## Pillar 1: Meal Preps

Prepared meals for weekly planning, wellness, convenience and family routines.

Examples:

- individual prepared meals,
- weekly meal bundles,
- corporate meal plans,
- family meals,
- healthier recurring meals,
- subscription-friendly meal selections.

## Pillar 2: Holiday Menus

Seasonal food offers for special dates and family/corporate celebrations.

Examples:

- Thanksgiving menu,
- Christmas dinner,
- New Year party food,
- Valentine's menu,
- Mother's Day / Father's Day menus,
- corporate holiday meals.

## Pillar 3: Party Boxes

Ready-to-share food boxes for small events, gatherings and celebrations.

Examples:

- appetizer boxes,
- sweets boxes,
- party snacks,
- dessert boxes,
- themed dinner boxes,
- small event food kits,
- celebration boxes.

## Technical Note

These pillars do not have to map one-to-one to database categories. They may be implemented as:

- store categories,
- CMS landing sections,
- tags/attributes,
- product filters,
- collections,
- homepage blocks,
- campaign pages.

Before changing category data, review the current `store_categories`, `store_products_categories`, product attributes and homepage sections.
